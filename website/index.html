Questions

Good to know

When can I download Flossi?

Flossi is coming soon to Google Play for Android phones. This page will link to the store listing as soon as it’s live.

Which currencies can I use?

You choose your currency during setup: Moroccan Dirham, Euro, US Dollar and more than 60 others. You can change it later in Settings.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Everything you do in Flossi works offline, and your data is stored on your phone.

How do I keep a copy of my data?

Go to Settings → Export transactions to save a CSV file of everything you’ve recorded. Keep it wherever you like.

Which languages are supported?

English, French and Arabic. Flossi can follow your phone’s language or you can pick one in Settings.
